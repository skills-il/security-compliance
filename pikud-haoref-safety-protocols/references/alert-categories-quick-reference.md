# Alert Categories Quick Reference

Note: Home Front Command issues alerts by named type. The "Cat" numbers below are the Tzofar / Tzevaadom feed codes used by community alert apps, not official HFC labels.

| Cat | Hebrew | English | Action | Shelter Time |
|-----|--------|---------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | ירי רקטות וטילים | Missiles/Rockets | Enter shelter, close door/window, sit below window line | Until an explicit Home Front Command instruction to leave. No timer, no 10-minute rule, for any threat type |
| 2 | חדירת כלי טיס עוין | Hostile Aircraft | Enter shelter, close all openings, stay away from windows | Until an explicit Home Front Command instruction to leave (same rule as rockets) |
| 3 | רעידת אדמה | Earthquake | Open ground within seconds; else mamad with door AND window OPEN; else stairwell down and out; else kneel, get low to the ground, protect head with hands. **NO lift, NO standing under a doorway, and NO underground shelter** (the opposite of a rocket alert). Wheelchair: lock it and protect your head | Until shaking stops + check structure |
| 4 | צונאמי | Tsunami | Move inland and to height; leave low buildings; see oref.org.il for the official distance and floor figures | Until official all-clear |
| 5 | אירוע רדיולוגי | Radiological | Enter building, seal room, activate NBC if available. NOTE: Pikud HaOref publishes NO guidance page for this category, so these steps are general practice, not official Israeli instructions. Follow live broadcasts and call 104 | Follow official instructions |
| 6 | חומרים מסוכנים | Hazardous Materials | Stay inside, close doors and windows, TURN OFF THE AIR CONDITIONER. Do not leave until an end-of-event announcement | Until an official end-of-event announcement |
| 7 | חדירת מחבלים | Terrorist Infiltration | Lock the door, LEAVE THE OUTSIDE LIGHTS ON, go to the mamad, sit below the window line, stay silent, do NOT open the door | Until an official end-of-event announcement |
| 13 | האירוע הסתיים | Event Concluded | You may leave the protected space. The ground outside is NOT declared safe: unexploded ordnance and interceptor debris can detonate on contact, so move away, keep others away and report to police 100. Do not gather at an impact site | N/A |
| 14 | התרעה מוקדמת | Pre-Alert | Prepare, move toward shelter | No official expiry window is published. Cat 14 is a community alert-app feed code, not an official HFC category. Never tell a user a pre-alert has "expired" |
