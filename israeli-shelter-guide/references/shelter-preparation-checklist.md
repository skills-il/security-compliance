# Shelter Preparation Checklist

## Mamad (Safe Room) Readiness

### Structural
- [ ] Blast door opens and closes fully
- [ ] Blast door hinges lubricated (annual)
- [ ] Window shutter closes and locks from inside
- [ ] Room is clear of obstacles blocking the door
- [ ] No heavy items stored on shelves above head height

### Supplies (per person)
- [ ] Water: 3 liters per person per day x 3 days = 9 liters minimum (Pikud Ha'Oref figure)
- [ ] Flashlight with fresh batteries
- [ ] Battery-operated radio, tuned to the relevant regional station. Pikud Ha'Oref names Kol BaRama (105.7 / 104.3 / 92.1 / 107.6 FM), Kol Chai (92.8 / 93 / 102.5 FM), Radio Darom (101.5 FM), Galei Yisrael (106.5 / 94 / 89.3 FM), and Kan Moreshet at weekends (90.5 / 90.8 / 92.5 / 100.7 FM)
- [ ] Phone charger and power bank (charged)
- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Personal medications (3-day supply)
- [ ] Copies of important documents in sealed bag
- [ ] Food that needs no refrigeration, for 3 days
- [ ] Water and food stock replaced and expiry checked every 3 months

### For Families with Children
- [ ] Books, games, coloring supplies
- [ ] Comfort items (stuffed animal, blanket)
- [ ] Extra diapers/formula if applicable
- [ ] Written emergency plan (who goes where, meeting points)

### For Elderly / Mobility-Limited
- [ ] Chair or cushion in the mamad
- [ ] Assistive devices accessible (walker, cane)
- [ ] Neighbor/building committee informed of needs
- [ ] Registered with Home Front Command (call 104, or SMS/WhatsApp 052-9104104)

## Annual Maintenance Schedule
- **Tishrei (September/October)**: Full mamad check before winter/security season
- **Nisan (March/April)**: Spring maintenance, check supplies expiration dates
- **Whenever security situation escalates**: Immediate readiness check
