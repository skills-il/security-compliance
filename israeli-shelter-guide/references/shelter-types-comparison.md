# Shelter Types Comparison

## Detailed Comparison

| Feature | Mamad (ממ"ד) | Mamak (ממ"ק) | Maman (ממ"מ) | Miklat (מקלט) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Full name** | מרחב מוגן דירתי | מרחב מוגן קומתי | מרחב מוגן מוסדי | מקלט ציבורי |
| **Location** | Inside apartment | Shared per floor | Institution/workplace | Underground/standalone |
| **Access** | Private (apartment key) | Shared (floor residents) | Institution-managed | Public (municipality) |
| **Typical size** | 9-15 sq m | 15-30 sq m | Varies by institution | 50+ sq m |
| **Required since** | 1992 (new construction) | Pre-1992 retrofits | All institutions | 1951 (Civil Defense Law) |
| **Maintained by** | Apartment owner | Building committee | Institution | Municipality |
| **NBC protection** | Connection point | Varies | Usually equipped | Usually equipped |
| **Blast door** | Steel, apartment-grade | Steel, floor-grade | Steel, institutional | Steel, heavy-duty |
| **Advantages** | Immediate access, private | No need to leave floor | Large capacity | Strongest protection |
| **Disadvantages** | Small, often used as storage | Shared, may be crowded | Limited to institution hours | May be far, may be locked |

## Protection Level Hierarchy (strongest to weakest)

1. Underground miklat (public shelter)
2. Mamad / Mamak / Maman (reinforced rooms)
3. Interior stairwell (2+ floors above, 1+ below)
4. Interior room (fewest exterior walls/windows)
5. Any enclosed space
6. Open area (lie flat, face down)

## Legal Framework

- **Civil Defense Law (1951)**: Established the miklat requirement
- **Amendment 1992**: Mandated mamad in all new residential construction
- **Israeli Standard T"Y 4422**: Technical specifications for protected spaces
- **Accessibility Law (2005)**: Public shelters must be wheelchair accessible
- **2025 Amendment**: Allows mamad expansion to 12 sq m + 3 sq m bathroom
