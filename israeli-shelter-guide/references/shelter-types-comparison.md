# Shelter Types Comparison

## Detailed Comparison

| Feature | Mamad (ממ"ד) | Mamak (ממ"ק) | Maman (ממ"מ) | Miklat (מקלט) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Full name** | מרחב מוגן דירתי | מרחב מוגן קומתי | מרחב מוגן מוסדי | מקלט ציבורי |
| **Location** | Inside apartment | Shared per floor | Institution/workplace | Underground/standalone |
| **Access** | Private (apartment key) | Shared (floor residents) | Institution-managed | Public (municipality) |
| **Typical size** | 9 sq m net minimum, 22.5 cubic metres | Varies | Varies by institution | Varies |
| **Required since** | 1992 (new residential construction, mamad or mamak) | Pre-1992 retrofits | Varies | Civil Defense Law, 1951 |
| **Maintained by** | Apartment owner | Building committee | Institution | Municipality |
| **NBC protection** | Connection point | Varies | Usually equipped | Usually equipped |
| **Blast door** | Steel, per its own approval plate | Steel | Steel | Steel |
| **Advantages** | Immediate access, private | No need to leave floor | Large capacity | Strongest protection |
| **Disadvantages** | Small, often used as storage | Shared, may be crowded | Limited to institution hours | May be far, may be locked |

## Protection Level Hierarchy (strongest to weakest)

1. Underground miklat (public shelter)
2. Mamad / Mamak / Maman (reinforced rooms)
3. Internal stairwell, no windows and no external walls. Over three storeys: a flight with at least two floors above it. Under three storeys: the middle floor's flight. Never the entrance floor
4. Interior room (fewest exterior walls/windows)
5. Any enclosed space
6. Open area (lie flat, face down)

## Legal Framework

- **חוק ההתגוננות האזרחית, התשי"א-1951**: the enabling statute for the whole civil-defence regime, including the shelter regulations below
- **תקנות ההתגוננות האזרחית (מפרטים לבניית מקלטים), התש"ן-1990**: the actual source of mamad area, volume, wall and ceiling thicknesses and ceiling height. Not IS 4422
- **Israeli Standard 4422**: blast door, blast window and frame items. Paywalled; do not quote clauses from secondary sources
- **חוק שוויון זכויות לאנשים עם מוגבלות, התשנ"ח-1998**: the accessibility framework statute. Shelter accessibility itself is in **תקנות ההתגוננות האזרחית (התאמות נגישות במקלטים), התשע"ו-2016**, made under sections 14ד and 27 of the 1951 law
- **A 2025 planning-law amendment enlarging the permitted mamad area is widely reported.** Its figures were not read in the amendment text during the 2026-08-19 review, so no number for it is stated here. Do not import one from a secondary source

## Mamad Dimensions (תקנות ההתגוננות האזרחית (מפרטים לבניית מקלטים), התש"ן-1990)

NOT from Israeli Standard 4422. IS 4422 covers the blast door, blast window and frame items only.

- **Minimum floor area**: 9 square meters net excluding walls, and a volume of not less than 22.5 cubic meters (תקנה 197א). Minimum width 1.60 m, and a mamad may not serve as a kitchen, bathroom or toilet (תקנות 198(ב), 199).
- **Hardship floor**: the competent authority may approve a smaller mamad where site engineering does not allow the standard one, but never below 5 sq m net and 12.5 cubic metres (תקנה 197א).
- **Bathroom allowance in a confrontation area**: under the permit-exemption route, a mamad in an אזור עימות may add up to 3 sq m of floor area serving as a toilet or bathroom (תקנה 30ב(1)). A 2025 planning-law amendment further enlarging the permitted mamad area is widely reported; do not quote a figure for it that you have not read in the amendment itself.
- **Walls**: reinforced concrete, not less than 25 cm on an external wall (30 cm where a sliding blast window is fitted), 20 cm on an internal wall, 15 cm on a wall shared with a neighbouring mamad (תקנה 208). Note that תקנה 201 is the mamad LOCATION rule, not the thickness rule; the two are routinely swapped in secondary sources.
- **Ceiling and floor**: not less than 15 cm, and not less than 20 cm for the ceiling of the topmost mamad and the floor of the bottom one.
- **Ceiling height**: not less than 2.5 m and not more than 2.8 m clear (תקנה 198(א)).
- **Door and window**: steel blast door and steel-frame blast window, both closing from inside. Thickness is a manufacturer specification, not a regulated figure. Do not quote a door thickness to a user; refer them to the door's own approval plate.
- **Protective wall (קיר מגן)**: reinforced concrete at least 20 cm thick, rising to 25 cm for a dedicated protected space in a health or education institution in a forward locality. The clearance between it and the door leaf is at least 110 cm in a mamad. Whether one is required in a given design, and any exemption, is for the licensing engineer to determine; do not tell a user they can omit it.
- **NBC filtration**: ventilation and filtering system connection point; turn on and set to "ventilation" mode when sheltering.

## Adding a Private Mamad: full procedure

**Check eligibility first.** The permit-exemption route in סימן ו'1 of תקנות התכנון והבנייה (עבודות ומבנים הפטורים מהיתר) is NOT a general route for any building without a mamad. It covers only a **low-rise dwelling unit** (building of no more than two storeys above ground) or a **ground-attached dwelling unit** (none above or below it), in each case one that has no mamad. An apartment in a five-storey pre-1992 block is not covered, and telling its owner otherwise sends them down a route that does not exist for them; that case goes through the ordinary building-permit process with the local planning committee.

The route is a temporary order running three years from 26.10.2023, already amended three times. Confirm its current status with the licensing authority rather than assuming expiry or extension. Where eligible:

1. Appoint a licensed architect, engineer, or building technician as the request author (orech bakasha).
2. Submit the application through Pikud Ha'Oref's licensing system at **oref-rishuy.org.il**, with the supporting documents the system lists.
3. The competent authority examines the request within 14 days of receipt and notifies the applicant of approval or rejection (תקנה 30ג(ב)).
4. A structural engineer must certify, after construction, that the mamad meets the civil-defence construction specifications and that construction did not damage the building's structural frame.
5. Notice of the completed work is given to the licensing authority and to the competent authority within 45 days of completion (תקנה 30ד).

Costs and exact procedures vary by municipality. Confirm directly with your local Vaadat HaTichnun (planning committee).
