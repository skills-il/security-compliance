# Scam patterns beyond the three national ones

SKILL.md Step 5 carries the three patterns the joint Bank of Israel, Israel Police and Ministry of
Communications task force focuses on (phone-number takeover, phishing sites, vishing and
smishing). This file carries the newer layers. Add a pattern here only with an official Israeli
source, or an official statement relayed verbatim by the press, and date it.

## AI voice cloning and deepfakes

- **A cloned voice** of a relative or a manager asking for an urgent transfer. The countermeasure is
  social and must be arranged in advance: a family code word for emergencies known only to the
  family, and a callback on the number you already have. The National Cyber Directorate's rule is
  "לא פועלים מתוך לחץ. מנתקים ומתקשרים לבן משפחה במספר המוכר", under the heading
  עוצרים, מנתקים, מאמתים: stop, hang up, verify. Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/us080726
- **Deepfake video calls.** Looking and sounding right is no longer evidence of identity.
- **Deepfake investment videos** using the faces of well-known Israeli finance figures. Attribute
  these warnings to מערך הסייבר, not to רשות ניירות ערך.

## Fake police letters (October 2026)

Emails and text messages posing as the cyber unit of להב 433, reported by Israel Police on 7.10.2026.
They claim the recipient viewed sexual material involving minors and threaten legal steps unless the recipient
replies to the email. Israel Police said: "אנו מבהירים כי מדובר בהודעת כזב וכי לא מדובר בפנייה
ממשטרת ישראל", and asked recipients "שלא להשיב לה, לא ללחוץ על קישורים המצורפים אליה ולא להוריד קבצים
למחשב".

Source: Israel Police statement as reported by Arutz 7, https://www.inn.co.il/news/707941 (the
primary police page was not located).

The threat is shameful, so a user may act before asking anyone. Tell them that Israel Police has
disowned these messages, and follow its instruction: do not reply, click or download. The report also
recommends blocking the sending address or number.

## Not added, and why

- QR codes stuck on parking meters or tickets (quishing) and fake traffic-fine SMS: reported in
  Israeli media and by a private security firm in October 2026, but no official Israeli warning for
  this period was located. Covered generically by Step 4 (never reach a payment page through a link
  or a code you did not navigate to yourself).
