# Domain Coverage Checklist : israeli-scam-detector

Generated: 2026-09-02 via research on: חוק שירותי תשלום תשע"ט-2019 (nevo full text), הוראת ניהול בנקאי תקין 426 / חוזר ח-06-2834 (BoI PDF, text layer), מערך הסייבר הלאומי (gov.il + official Telegram channel), בנק ישראל, משרד התקשורת החלטת שימוע 03.06.2020, רשות המסים, ביטוח לאומי, רשות הדואר, כל-זכות.

Scope: help a non-technical Israeli user decide whether a suspicious SMS, email, link or phone call is a scam, and tell them exactly what to do next, including after the money is already gone. Audience explicitly includes elderly Israelis, Russian-speaking Israelis, and the family members who help them.

**Verification status.** Every figure, deadline and section number in the Must-cover money rows was read from primary text. Rows marked TELEGRAM-SOURCED or PRESS-SOURCED must be attributed in the skill exactly as marked, never as "gov.il says".

## Must cover (core)

### A. The governing money rule

- [ ] **A1. חוק כרטיסי חיוב, התשמ"ו-1986 is REPEALED.** s.55 of חוק שירותי תשלום, תשע"ט-2019: "חוק כרטיסי חיוב, התשמ"ו-1986 - בטל". Governing instrument since 14.10.2020 (s.77(a)); s.78(a) applies it retroactively to instruments issued earlier. Source: https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/502_043.htm - why core: nearly every Israeli guide still cites the dead statute; a user quoting it to a bank is quoting repealed law.
- [ ] **A2. The liability ladder, s.24.** (ב) zero liability for misuse after notice. (ג) before notice the payer owes the LOWER of: ₪75 fixed + ₪30 per day from the day the payer learned, capped at ₪450 if notice given within 30 days of first misuse; OR the amount actually transacted. Notice day excluded from the count if notice given the same day of discovery. Source: s.24(ב)-(ג) - why core: this is the number the user needs in the first minute, and the ₪30/day clock runs from DISCOVERY, which is what makes "call now" load-bearing.
- [ ] **A3. "הודעה" means notice to the payment service provider, NOT the police.** The word משטרה appears nowhere in the statute. Source: s.24(א) - why core: users burn days at a police station while the ₪30/day clock runs.
- [ ] **A4. Refund deadline is 8 business days**, not 30. s.27(א): "בהקדם האפשרי אך לא יאוחר משמונה ימי עסקים" from the day of notice. Source: s.27(א) - why core: the widely-repeated 30 days concedes three weeks the user is entitled to.
- [ ] **A5. The bank may re-debit but owes 15 days' written warning** with reasons, plus copies of its documents on request (s.27(ב)); simultaneous notice only where the payer acted fraudulently. Source: s.27(ב) - why core: users treat the refund as final and get ambushed; the 15-day window is their opening to contest.
- [ ] **A6. The cap is LOST under s.24(ד)** if the payer made the רכיב חיוני available to another person, "בין שהשימוש נעשה בידיעת המשלם ובין שנעשה שלא בידיעתו". Only carve-outs: handed over for safekeeping only / to a מוטב to initiate payment, and then stolen or lost by that person. **Deception is NOT a listed carve-out.** Source: s.24(ד) - why core: this is the vishing victim's exact situation.
- [ ] **A7. UNRESOLVED, must be framed as unresolved.** No case law located on whether a user deceived into disclosing an OTP falls under s.24(ד). The skill states the statutory text, states plainly that the point is untested, and NEVER tells the user whether they are covered. It still gives the unambiguous action: notify the bank now. - why core: a confident answer either way would be both unsourced and harmful.
- [ ] **A8. No liability at all if the provider gave no reasonable way to give notice** (s.24(ה)) - reinforced by B1 below. - why core: converts the user's "nobody answered" frustration into a statutory argument.
- [ ] **A9. Payer fraud voids everything** (s.26), and the law cannot be contracted out of except in the customer's favour (s.51). Source: ss.26, 51 - why core: sets the honest floor and pre-empts "but I signed the bank's terms".

### B. Who to contact, in order

- [ ] **B1. First call is the bank / card issuer, not the police.** Since 31.5.2026, הוראת ניהול בנקאי תקין 426 obliges every banking corporation to give a professional HUMAN phone answer for suspected payment fraud, "בכל שעות היממה ובכל ימות השבוע, לרבות בשעות המנוחה השבועית". Wait normally under 6 minutes; no more than 15% of monthly fraud calls may exceed 6 minutes, and those must average under 8. Source: https://www.boi.org.il/media/k23b3lzq/h2834.pdf - why core: the single most time-critical action, now with an enforceable service standard the user can cite. Shabbat coverage matters because families otherwise wait until Sunday while the clock runs.
- [ ] **B2. Senior citizens get queue priority on the fraud line.** Directive 426 purpose clause: "מענה טלפוני אנושי מקצועי שיסייע ללקוחות בתחום ההונאות, **לתת קדימות בתור לאזרחים ותיקים**". Source: ibid. - why core: a concrete, demandable entitlement absent from every consumer guide reviewed.
- [ ] **B3. Russian-language service on the fraud line.** Directive 426: "מתן השירות, בין היתר, **בשפה הרוסית**, וזאת לאור העובדה **שתופעת ההונאות שכיחה בקרב דוברי השפה הרוסית**". Explanatory notes confirm this was added after public consultation: "עלה הצורך לתת דגש לדוברי השפה הרוסית, לאור שכיחות התופעה בקרב אוכלוסייה זו". Source: ibid. - why core: the regulator itself identifies Russian speakers as a disproportionately targeted population and creates a service right for them.
- [ ] **B4. 119 (מערך הסייבר) is a CERT, not a recovery mechanism.** Initial triage, guidance, infrastructure takedown. Its own page states it does not replace a police report, and it does not handle computer faults. Source: https://www.gov.il/he/service/cyber-event-report - why core: two mirror-image errors are common ("119 gets your money back" and "skip 119, just call police"); the answer is both, bank first.
- [ ] **B5. Police: the online form, or the station nearest the area of residence; 100 in an emergency.** The online filing is a בקשה, not a completed complaint, and the user may still be summoned. Source: https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-file-complaint-online-during-emergency - why core: sets honest expectations about what filing achieves and when.
- [ ] **B6. מוקד 105 is for minors (0-18) only.** Not the adult scam line, despite "הונאה" appearing in its intake list. Adults: 100 and 119. - why core: a very common and time-wasting misroute.

### C. Identification: what actually proves a message is fake

- [ ] **C1. A text sender name proves NOTHING.** משרד התקשורת decision 03.06.2020, הנחת יסוד 4(ג): "המשרד לא רואה בשימוש באותיות לועזיות בשדה ה-CLI של מסרון זיהוי חד-חד ערכי של מנוי. לכן, ראוי שמשתמש קצה המקבל מסרון כששם השולח הוא טקסטואלי יפעיל שיקול דעת בהקשר לאמינות מקור המסרון." The Ministry further replied that "זיוף" is not even the relevant concept for alphabetic CLI. Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/03062020_2 - why core: demolishes the single most widely repeated piece of advice. SCOPE CAREFULLY to the 2020 anti-spoofing decision; a 2022 hearing does reach textual CLI but no gov.il original was located, so do not state the current across-the-board position.
- [ ] **C2. A forged SMS can appear INSIDE the genuine thread.** Three documented instances on the National Cyber Directorate's official Telegram channel: GOV.IL (t.me/Israel_Cyber/386), כביש 6 (/366), פנגו (/382). INCD's own diagnosis: "מדובר בניצול של אופן הצגת הודעות SMS במכשירים מסוימים, ולא בפריצה למערכות GOV.IL". **TELEGRAM-SOURCED** - no gov.il permalink exists; attribute to the Directorate's official channel, never to gov.il. - why core: thread position is the last heuristic most users trust, and it is worthless.
- [ ] **C3. The only reliable test is the one the user initiates.** Navigate to the site or app yourself, or call the number on the back of your own card. Never a number, link or button supplied by the message. - why core: this is the skill's single organising principle; every identification rule reduces to it.
- [ ] **C4. Domain rules that DO hold, per each body's own page.** רשות הדואר: "בהודעות אמיתיות מרשות הדואר תופיע רק אחת משתי כתובות אינטרנט: https://postil.co.il, https://israelpost.co.il. כל שינוי בכתובת, משמעותו שמדובר במתחזים" (https://doar.israelpost.co.il/content/fishing). ביטוח לאומי: "סיום כתובת האינטרנט שלנו תמיד תהיה Gov.il" (https://www.btl.gov.il/About/newspapers/Pages/hodaaitonotfakesaagetari2026.aspx). - why core: a domain check is falsifiable; a sender-name check is not.
- [ ] **C5. "Look for gov.il and you're safe" is FALSE.** MyVisit is on myvisit.com; רשות המסים has no own domain; בנק יהב is hyphenated; דואר ישראל legitimately uses a doar. subdomain. - why core: the naive domain heuristic produces false positives on legitimate services.
- [ ] **C6. Never publish a list of legitimate sender IDs.** Per C1 this teaches exactly the check that spoofing defeats. Explicitly out of scope and the reason must be stated. - why core: a well-intentioned feature that would make users less safe.

### D. The 2026 AI-driven wave

- [ ] **D1. Cloned-voice calls.** The verification protocol is a pre-agreed family code word plus a callback on a number you already hold, never the number that called. Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/us080726 ("עוצרים. מנתקים. מאמתים.") - why core: the countermeasure is social, not technical, and must be set up BEFORE the call.
- [ ] **D2. Deepfake video calls.** INCD's biometric unit has warned that a person met on a video call may be fake "גם אם הוא נראה ונשמע אמיתי לחלוטין". Any percentage figure for the rise in deepfake fraud is PRESS-SOURCED and was not verified against a primary source; do not state one. **Attribution:** INCD warned about the TECHNIQUE; the Israeli voice-cloned-CEO incident is PRESS-SOURCED only, and must be marked as such or omitted. - why core: video is the last identity proof users trust.
- [ ] **D3. Deepfake investment videos impersonating real Israeli finance figures.** Attribute to **מערך הסייבר**, NOT to רשות ניירות ערך. - why core: a mis-attribution here was caught during research and would have been published as fact.

### E. Targeting: who is actually hit (all three are primary-sourced)

- [ ] **E1. Low financial and digital literacy is the documented focus.** Joint BoI / משטרת ישראל / משרד התקשורת task force, 15.4.2026: "פעולות ההונאה מכוונות לפגוע בכלל ציבור הלקוחות, אולם ניכרת התמקדות באוכלוסיות בעלות התמצאות פיננסית ודיגיטלית נמוכה". Source: https://www.boi.org.il/publications/pressreleases/15-4-26a/ - why core: grounds the elderly and Russian-speaker lanes in a regulatory finding rather than an impression.
- [ ] **E2. The three named national fraud patterns**, same source, verbatim: השתלטות על מספר הטלפון של הלקוח (SIM Swap ו"עקוב אחריי"); דיוג ("Phishing") באמצעות אתרים המתחזים לגופי המערכת הבנקאית; הונאות טלפוניות ("Vishing") והונאות באמצעות הודעות SMS ("Smishing"). - why core: gives the skill a sourced top-level triage tree.
- [ ] **E3. SIM swap tell.** Carriers changed the porting (ניוד) SMS to state the customer's intent plainly and push the OTP to the END of the message. Practical rule: an unrequested porting SMS is the attack in progress; sudden loss of cellular signal is the confirmation. Source: ibid. - why core: SIM swap defeats SMS-OTP entirely and has a narrow, recognisable warning window.

### F. Aftermath

- [ ] **F1. Post-incident checklist**: block the card, notify BOTH bank and card company, hunt for unfamiliar new הוראות קבע, enable purchase alerts, change passwords, enable 2FA. Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/fool_me_twice - why core: the new-standing-orders step is the one users skip and the one that bleeds money for months.
- [ ] **F2. Unauthorised הרשאה לחיוב has a much faster clock**: cancel a specific debit within 3 business days of the charge, refund within 1 business day (s.35); cancel the mandate any time (s.34); over-charge refunded within 1 business day (s.37). Source: ss.34-37 - why core: a distinct and far shorter deadline than s.24.
- [ ] **F3. Stolen ת"ז does NOT require a police complaint.** A תצהיר (מר/15) suffices per נוהל 2.18.0004. The replacement fee changes and was NOT verified against a primary source this cycle; do not state a figure, route the user to רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה for the current fee. - why core: a widely believed false precondition.
- [ ] **F4. A US-style "fraud alert" does not exist in Israel.** Route the user to the credit data register (מאגר נתוני אשראי) at the Bank of Israel to ask what options exist. The specific statutory mechanism under חוק נתוני אשראי was NOT verified against the statute text this cycle, so the skill must not cite a section number. - why core: users import a US mechanism that has no Israeli equivalent, and a wrong section number here causes lasting self-inflicted damage.

### G. Framing and safety

- [ ] **G1. Never say a specific message is safe.** When the skill cannot confirm, it must still give the action: stop, do not click, and verify by navigating yourself or calling the number on your card. - why core: repo rule, refusal is not a stop sign.
- [ ] **G2. Answering a scam call does not infect the phone.** INCD during the Sept 2025 robocall wave: "עצם המענה לשיחה לא גורם לנזק לטלפון". - why core: defuses panic, especially for elderly users who then avoid all calls including the bank's callback.
- [ ] **G3. Never pay to recover a hijacked account or to stop sextortion.** INCD: "לא משלמים - מדווחים. האקר לא מתכוון להחזיר את החשבון." - why core: payment marks the victim for repeat targeting.
- [ ] **G4. Legal notice required.** The skill states a user's statutory liability, deadlines and what a bank owes them: a conclusion about the user's own legal position. Needs `## הבהרה משפטית` / `## Legal notice` immediately after the H1 in both language files, and a legal clause OPENING the description. - why core: repo legal-review gate.

## Should cover (advanced / edge)

- [ ] S1. WhatsApp account hijack - listed by INCD as a reportable incident category on its reporting service. Very common in Israel; the hijacked account then scams the victim's contacts. INCD's dedicated guidance page would not render (Cloudflare) and is therefore NOT cited; report via 119.
- [ ] S2. Investment fraud / Ponzi - the decision tree is "is this entity licensed", not "is this link fake". ISA scams hub.
- [ ] S3. Impersonation of authorities, including forged תעודת שוטר, letters "מטעם בנק ישראל", a fake מערך הסייבר website harvesting card details, and fake פיקוד העורף alerts during wartime.
- [ ] S4. רשות המסים impersonation - fake SMS/email with a link to a page impersonating the Authority's digital services, and emails titled "התראה על פתיחת חקירה רשמית". Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/pa010825-1
- [ ] S5. ביטוח לאומי impersonation - fake war-grant messages. BTL's own rules: real notice also appears in the אזור אישי under 'מכתבים'; verify via *6050 or the human chat; and "הביטוח הלאומי לא מבקש פרטי אשראי כדי להעביר תגמולים". Source: BTL page above.
- [ ] S6. Sextortion - never pay; different, non-financial response path; no-shame tone.
- [ ] S7. הרשות להגנת הצרכן - real route, but scoped to acts against a BUSINESS, not an anonymous criminal. State the limit or it wastes the user's time.
- [ ] S8. הרשות להגנת הפרטיות - the address for the body that LEAKED your data, not for your own remedy; its enforcement is "בגין הפרת החוק באופן כללי ולא כסעד פרטני לאדם המתלונן". Civil limitation 2 years.
- [ ] S9. Reporting for takedown - INCD's form wants a screenshot, the URL/IP, and the original message. There is no dedicated field for "report this WhatsApp/SMS number".
- [ ] S10. Business-lane pretexts (Zoom invitation, job offer, partnership) for the freelancer slice of the audience.
- [ ] S11. Pending reform: a תזכיר חוק שירותי תשלום (תיקון), התשפ"ו-2026 is circulating aimed at the deceived-transfer gap. Must be labelled "not yet law".

## Out of scope (explicit)

- **Naming specific banks' fraud phone numbers.** Deliberately excluded: numbers drift, and printing a list trains the exact "trust the number in front of you" reflex C3 exists to break. Teach "find it yourself on the bank's site or the back of your card".
- **Publishing a list of legitimate government SMS sender names.** See C6. No consolidated list was found and publishing one would be actively harmful.
- **Cross-border civil recovery once money has left Israel.** Plausibly asked, so it gets a real answer: no Israeli primary source describes a recovery route specific to cross-border scam losses. Say so, route to police + bank + a lawyer (free legal aid for 65+, senior hotline *8840). A confident procedural claim here would be actively harmful.
- **Whether an insurance claim requires a police report.** Plausibly asked. Unsourced for online fraud; the norm exists for physical theft and must not be extended. Answer: not established, ask your insurer for its written requirement.
- **Technical malware removal, forensics, device reimaging.** 119 states it does not handle computer faults. Route out.
- **Reporting on behalf of a minor victim.** One line plus מוקד 105, not a branch.
- **Criminal-procedure guidance** - what police will investigate, charging decisions. The professional's lane.
- **Business/enterprise incident response** (BEC playbooks, corporate IR). Different audience and severity tier.
