# The day after: aftermath checklist

Run this after the bank has been notified (Step 1A) or the recall requested (Step 1B), never
instead of it.

- Block the card, and notify **both** the bank and the card company.
- Hunt for **unfamiliar new standing orders** (הוראות קבע) and direct debits. This is the step people
  skip, and it bleeds money for months. A charge under a הרשאה לחיוב can be cancelled by notice
  within 3 business days of the charge (s.35 of חוק שירותי תשלום), so look now, not at month end.
  Older charges are not forfeited: the mandate itself can be cancelled at any time (s.34), a mandate
  set up without enhanced authentication or consent is invalid (s.33(4)), and a charge outside the
  mandate's terms is refunded within 1 business day (s.37).
- **Sweep for credit taken in the user's name.** Anyone holding a one-time code and account access
  routinely goes further than a single charge: check for a new digital loan, a raised card limit, a
  newly enrolled device, and whether the phone number or email on the account was changed. Force a
  device de-enrolment and a full credentials reset.
- **Preserve evidence before tidying up.** Do not delete the SMS, the call log or the chat.
  Screenshot the transaction, the beneficiary details and the calling number. Get the bank's
  confirmation of your notice **in writing**, by email or in-app message.
- Turn on purchase alerts, change reused passwords, enable two-factor authentication.
- If ID details were exposed, note that replacing a stolen תעודת זהות does **not** require a police
  complaint; a תצהיר suffices (נוהל 2.18.0004). No Israeli equivalent of a US fraud alert was found,
  so ask the credit data register (מאגר נתוני אשראי) at the Bank of Israel what options actually
  exist here. Do not state a replacement fee: it was not verified.
- If the bank froze the user's own card or app (s.23 allows it where law requires it or the contract
  allows it, for reasonable grounds such as suspected misuse or fraud), s.23 requires notice and reasons (before the freeze,
  or as soon as possible after where notice would defeat its purpose), written confirmation of an
  oral notice, and lifting the freeze or issuing a replacement instrument once the reasons end.

Source for the standing-orders and card steps: מערך הסייבר הלאומי, "אחרי האירוע",
https://www.gov.il/he/pages/fool_me_twice
