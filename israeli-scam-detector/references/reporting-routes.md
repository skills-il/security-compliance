# Reporting routes: what each one actually does

Order matters. The bank is always first, because it is the only route that starts a statutory
clock. Everything else can be done afterwards without losing money.

## 1. The bank or card issuer

**Directive 426 binds a תאגיד בנקאי only** (s.3(א); s.3(ב) repealed). The 24/7 human answer, the six-minute standard and senior queue priority are demandable from a bank. A standalone card issuer (Max, Isracard, CAL) is not an addressee of 426, though the Payment Services Law duties in ss.24 and 27 still apply to it as a payment service provider.

**Always first.** This is "הודעה" under s.24 of חוק שירותי תשלום, and it is the switch that stops
liability accruing.

- Liability for misuse after notice is zero (s.24(ב)).
- Before notice it accrues at ₪75 plus ₪30 per day from the day the person found out.
- The refund is due within 8 business days of notice (s.27(א)).

Since 31 May 2026 the line must be human, professional, and available around the clock every day of
the week including the weekly rest hours, with a wait normally under six minutes. Customers aged 70 or over
are owed priority in the queue (s.7, and it covers a card the banking corporation itself issued).
Russian-language service is NOT in the directive's operative text, only in its explanatory notes as
an example under Directive 501: ask for it, do not demand it.

**How to find the number:** the back of the card, or the bank's own app or website that you opened
yourself. Never the number in the message.

**What to record:** the exact time of notice, the reference number, the representative's name.

## 2. 119, מערך הסייבר הלאומי

The national CERT. Report the malicious link or number so the infrastructure can be taken down and
the next person does not lose money.

**It does:** initial triage, guidance, and takedown of malicious infrastructure. It also runs a
reporting form and a WhatsApp bot.

**It does not:** recover your money, replace a police report, or fix computer faults.

The reporting form asks for a screenshot, the URL or IP, and the original message. There is no
dedicated field for reporting a WhatsApp or SMS number, so include it in the free text.

### Hijacked WhatsApp account, and why nobody pays to get one back

The Cyber Directorate's own page on WhatsApp account takeover (updated 24.04.2025) documents a
recovery route that involves **no payment to anyone**. Paying an account hijacker or a sextortion
demand buys nothing and marks the user for repeat targeting. Never advise it.

The documented route, in order, and speed matters because it is a race against the attacker
enabling two-step verification first:

1. **Re-verify the number.** Enter the phone number on the app's login screen and confirm with the
   six-digit SMS or voice code. INCD: *"קודם כל, אסור לכם להשתהות: אם אין לכם אימות דו-שלבי, אתם
   צריכים לפעול לפני שהתוקף מפעיל אותו בעצמו"*.
2. **Immediately set two-step verification** (הגדרות > חשבון > אימות דו שלבי) and a recovery email.
3. **If that fails**, the attacker has already set two-step verification. Submit a hijack report to
   WhatsApp.
4. **Warn contacts and groups** by another channel. The account is used mainly to run the same code
   trick on the victim's contacts and to solicit money in their name.
5. **Change the Google / iCloud password** where the chat backup lives.

Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/whatsapp_stolen_accounts (bot-blocked to curl; rendered in a
browser on 2026-09-02).

## 3. Police, 100 or the online form

Opens a criminal case. In an emergency call 100. Otherwise file online, or at **the police station nearest your area of residence**. Property and fraud offences are a listed category on the form.

**Understand what the online filing is:** it is a בקשה to file a complaint, not a completed
complaint. It is not official until investigators process it, and you may still be summoned to a
station.

**It does not** freeze a transfer. No primary source supports the belief that reporting to the
police stops money moving. That is the bank's lane, and only if you reach them in time.

A police report is useful evidence, and a bank may ask for a reference number as internal procedure,
but no clock in חוק שירותי תשלום depends on it.

## 4. מוקד 105

Part of יחידה 105 under להב 433, run by המשרד לביטחון לאומי / המטה הלאומי להגנה על ילדים ברשת.

**The VICTIM must be a minor, and the harm must be online.** Its published intake list ends with
"התייעצות ומידע על גלישה בטוחה ופגיעות בקטינים (גילאי 18-0)", so the 0 to 18 range is explicit.

**Do not tell an adult caller they may not call.** The page invites "ילדים, בני נוער, הורים, אנשי
מקצוע וכלל הציבור", so a parent or professional reporting harm to a child is a proper caller. What is
out of scope is an adult reporting harm to *themselves*. The word הונאה does appear in the intake
list, under "פגיעות פליליות ברשת - סחיטה, איומים, התחזות, הונאה", but the governing clause is "בכל
נושא של פגיעה בקטינים ברשת", so it is scoped to minors. That is why adult scam victims misroute here.

An adult scammed in their own right uses 100 and 119. Note the 105 page itself names only 100:
"שימו לב: במקרה של חשש לחיי אדם, יש להתקשר מיד למוקד 100 של משטרת ישראל." 

## 5. הרשות להגנת הצרכן

A real route, with a real limit: it acts against a **business**, not against an anonymous criminal.
Useful where an identifiable Israeli business misled or charged without authorisation. Complaints
may be filed anonymously, and forms exist in Arabic, English and Russian.

If the counterparty is an anonymous scammer, this route will not help and will cost time.

## 6. הרשות להגנת הפרטיות

This is the address for **the body that leaked your data**, not a route to your own remedy. Its
published process states that enforcement is undertaken for breach of the law generally
"ולא כסעד פרטני לאדם המתלונן".

**Correction, 2026-09-02.** Earlier versions of this file said the civil limitation period under
חוק הגנת הפרטיות is two years. That is wrong and it was wrong in the user's disfavour. Section 26 of
that law, headed התיישנות, now reads simply "26. (בוטל)" - it was repealed. No special limitation
period survives in the Privacy Protection Law. A civil claim is therefore governed by the ordinary
rule in חוק ההתיישנות, תשי"ח-1958 s.5(1), "בשאינו מקרקעין - שבע שנים", running from the day the cause
of action arose (s.6). **Seven years, not two.** Never tell a user their privacy claim has expired at
two years. If limitation is actually in issue, send them to a lawyer rather than computing it here.

## 7. Bank of Israel public enquiries

Where to complain about **the bank's own conduct**, as distinct from the fraud. Relevant if the
fraud line did not answer, if the bank refused the eight-business-day refund, or if it re-debited
without the fifteen days' written notice required by s.27(ב).

## What no route does

No Israeli primary source describes a civil recovery route specific to cross-border scam losses.
Once money has left the country, the honest answer is that recovery is difficult and there is no
published procedure to point to. Route the user to the bank, the police, and a lawyer. Any senior
citizen over 65 who fell victim to exploitation or economic abuse may approach the Ministry of
Justice legal aid for free advice and representation, at *6405.

Do not invent a procedure here. A confident but wrong answer sends a person who has already lost
money on a second, futile errand.
